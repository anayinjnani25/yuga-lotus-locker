The four yugas

Satya YugaThe Age of Truth

Gold coin. 1,728,000 years. Dharma stands on 4 legs. Avatars: Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha.

Treta YugaThe Age of Yajna

Silver coin. 1,296,000 years. Dharma on 3 legs. Avatars: Vamana, Parashurama, Shri Ram.

Dwapara YugaThe Age of Devotion

Copper coin. 864,000 years. Dharma on 2 legs. Avatars: Shri Krishna, Balarama.

Kali YugaThe Age of Darkness

Iron coin. 432,000 years. Dharma on 1 leg. Avatar to come: Kalki.
